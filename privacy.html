Privacy Policy

Satisfying Factory · Last updated November 2, 2026

Satisfying Factory is a private automation tool. It is used only by the owner of the Satisfying Factory channels to publish the owner's own videos and to review how those videos perform. It is not offered to the public and does not ask anyone else to sign in.

Information the app uses

When the owner authorizes the app with their own Google account, the app can:

  • upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel (YouTube Data API, upload permission);
  • read the owner's own channel and video information (YouTube Data API, read-only);
  • read the owner's own YouTube Analytics reports (YouTube Analytics API, read-only).

When the owner authorizes the app with their own Meta account, it can publish Reels to the owner's Instagram professional account and to the owner's Facebook Page, and read the insights of that account and that Page.

The app does not access data belonging to other people, and it does not collect information from viewers.

How the information is used and stored

The data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to produce performance summaries for the owner. Authorization tokens are kept in the owner's private cloud environment. Performance numbers are kept in the owner's private dashboard. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Satisfying Factory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions from Instagram's settings under apps and websites, and from Facebook's settings under Business integrations. Revoking access stops the app immediately; stored performance summaries can be deleted by the owner.

Deleting data

The app keeps no personal data about anyone other than the owner: only the owner's authorization tokens and the performance numbers of the owner's own videos. To delete them, remove the app as described above; the owner then deletes the stored tokens and dashboard entries. Anyone who believes the app holds data about them can ask for it to be deleted through the contact below, and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the channel owner through the BananaCat YouTube channel.
